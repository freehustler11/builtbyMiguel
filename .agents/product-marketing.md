## 3. Pain Points & Problems

- **Agencies with Junior Staff:** Paying traditional marketing agencies $3,000/mo for junior account managers who send 40-page PDF reports filled with vanity impressions that yield zero booked jobs.
- **Bloated Slow Websites:** Outdated WordPress sites built on heavy visual page builders that take 4-7 seconds to load on mobile, causing 50%+ bounce rates.
- **Shared Lead Traps:** Burning money on Angi, Yelp, and HomeAdvisor where the same lead is sold to 5 competing contractors simultaneously.
- **Slow Response Times:** Losing jobs because form fills sit in unmonitored email inboxes for hours instead of triggering instant SMS alerts.
